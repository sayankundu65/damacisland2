Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026. How we protect and manage your personal information.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how information collected through the Damac Island 2 informational portal (accessible at https://damacisland2.vercel.app) is gathered, processed, and safeguarded in accordance with applicable laws, including UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection (PDPL) and international data privacy best practices.

2. Information We Collect

When you request property details, floor plans, pricing, or callback consultations on our portal, we collect:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, telephone/WhatsApp number, and postal/PIN code.
  • Property Preferences: Preferred collection (e.g. 4BR, 5BR, or 6BR villas), investment intent (primary residence, holiday home, rental yield, Golden Visa), and indicative budget range.
  • Technical Information: IP address, device type, browser metadata, and referral source used to ensure site functionality and security.

3. Purpose of Processing

We process your personal information strictly to:

  • Fulfill your explicit request for property specifications, floor plans, and commercial terms.
  • Facilitate direct consultations with authorized DAMAC property specialists.
  • Transmit official project updates, construction milestones, and availability notices.
  • Prevent fraudulent submissions and maintain site performance.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data submitted through our inquiry forms is transmitted securely to authorized sales representatives and official property databases for the sole purpose of responding to your request.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption, SSL protocols, and restricted access mechanisms to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the inquiry or as required by regulatory compliance.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under the UAE PDPL and relevant data protection laws, you retain the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise your rights, contact us at dubai@damacgroup.com.

7. Updates to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
