About Damac Island 2

The vision, architecture, and developer credentials behind Dubai's most anticipated waterfront master community.

The Vision: Paradise as a Way of Living

Damac Island 2 is conceived as a transformative residential paradise that brings together the spirit, beauty, and atmosphere of eight world-renowned island escapes: Bahamas, Tahiti, Bermuda, Barbados, Cuba, Maui, Mauritius, and Antigua. Rather than offering traditional suburban living, Damac Island 2 weaves crystalline water lagoons, white-sand beaches, and lush tropical flora into every neighborhood cluster.

Master Developer: DAMAC Properties

Since its founding in 2002, DAMAC Properties has been at the forefront of the Middle East's luxury real estate market, delivering iconic residential, commercial, and leisure properties across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Jordan, Lebanon, Iraq, the Maldives, Canada, and the United Kingdom.

Five Core Lifestyle Pillars

Every element of Damac Island 2 is designed around five foundational wellness and lifestyle pillars:

  • Nature: Verdant waterways, Zen gardens, and the Horizon meditation trail.
  • Architecture: Contemporary coastal architecture featuring expansive glass facades, private plots, and natural stone textures.
  • Life & Soul: Island Vida central hub, floating market, and outdoor waterfront dining.
  • Wellness: Natural hot springs spa, hydrotherapy wellness pools, and aroma therapy gardens.
  • Adventure: The Aqua Dome, Iguana Park, jungle river, kayaking, and paddleboarding.

Investment & Golden Visa Benefits

Dubai is globally recognized for offering zero percent personal income tax, 100% foreign business ownership, high capital appreciation, and strong rental yields. Purchases of AED 2,000,000 (~₹4.5+ Cr) and above at Damac Island 2 qualify eligible investors and their families for the 10-Year UAE Golden Visa.
